# Activity Resize UAT

## Shorten

1. Press an activity.
2. Drag up.
   - Check that the activity is getting shorter.
   - Check that future activities are shifting earlier.
3. Release pointer.

## Lengthen

1. Press an activity.
2. Drag down.
   - Check that the activity is getting longer.
   - Check that future activities are shifting later.
3. Release pointer.

## Snap

1. Press an activity.
2. Drag to the left side where hour markers are.
   - Check that the activity's end time has snapped to the hour that is closest to the pointer coordinates.
3. Release pointer.
